FREELANCE CONTRACT

This Freelance Contract is made on 18 July 2026 between Lumen Studio (the "Client") and Kabir Anand (the "Freelancer"). The Freelancer will design and deliver a brand identity package for the Client.

1. SCOPE AND PAYMENT

1.1 The Freelancer shall deliver the work described in the project brief by 15 August 2026.

1.2 The Client shall pay a fixed project fee of INR 80,000. The Client shall pay thirty percent (30%) as an advance on signing and the balance of seventy percent (70%) on delivery of the final files.

1.3 The Client shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of receiving it. Overdue amounts accrue a late fee of 1.5% per month.

1.4 The project includes two (2) rounds of revisions. Further revisions are charged at INR 1,500 per hour.

1.5 If the Freelancer delivers late, the Freelancer shall pay a penalty of 1% of the project fee for each day of delay.

2. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

2.1 Ownership of the final work transfers to the Client only after the Freelancer has received full payment.

2.2 The Freelancer may show the work in a portfolio unless the Client objects in writing.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

3.1 Either party may terminate this Contract by giving the other party fourteen (14) days written notice.

3.2 If the Client cancels the project after work has started, the Client shall pay the Freelancer a cancellation fee of 25% of the unpaid project fee, plus payment for work already completed.

3.3 If a party breaches this Contract, the other party shall give written notice and the breaching party has seven (7) days to cure the breach before termination takes effect.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND LIABILITY

4.1 The Freelancer shall keep the Client's confidential information secret for two (2) years after this Contract ends.

4.2 The Freelancer shall indemnify the Client against third-party claims that the delivered work infringes intellectual property rights.

4.3 The Freelancer's total liability is limited to the project fee.

5. GENERAL

5.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of India and the courts of Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute.

5.2 The Freelancer is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Client.

5.3 This Contract may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.
