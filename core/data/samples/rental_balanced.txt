RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made on 10 January 2026 between Sunrise Estates Pvt Ltd (the "Landlord") and Karthik Rao (the "Tenant") for the house at 7 Palm Grove, Whitefield, Bengaluru (the "Premises").

1. TERM AND RENT

1.1 The tenancy begins on 1 February 2026 for a fixed term of eleven (11) months.

1.2 The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of INR 25,000 on or before the first day of each month.

1.3 If rent is not paid within five (5) days of the due date, the Tenant shall pay a flat late fee of INR 500.

1.4 The Landlord may increase the rent by up to 7% at renewal, after giving the Tenant sixty (60) days written notice.

1.5 After the fixed term, this Agreement continues month to month unless either party gives thirty (30) days written notice that it will not continue.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT

2.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to two (2) months' rent.

2.2 The Landlord shall return the deposit within thirty (30) days after the Tenant vacates, less documented costs of damage beyond normal wear and tear.

3. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

3.1 The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs and major systems. The Tenant is responsible for minor repairs and for any damage caused by the Tenant or the Tenant's guests.

3.2 The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection or repairs after giving at least twenty-four (24) hours notice.

3.3 The Tenant shall not sublet the Premises without the Landlord's written consent.

4. TERMINATION

4.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other party sixty (60) days written notice.

4.2 If the Tenant ends the tenancy during the fixed term, the Tenant shall pay an early termination fee equal to one (1) month's rent.

4.3 If a party breaches this Agreement, the other party may give written notice, and the breaching party has fifteen (15) days to cure the breach. If the breach is not cured, the notifying party may terminate this Agreement.

5. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

5.1 Each party shall indemnify the other against claims and losses arising from its own negligence or breach of this Agreement.

5.2 The Tenant shall maintain contents insurance for the Tenant's own belongings.

6. GENERAL

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Karnataka. Any dispute shall be referred to a single arbitrator in Bengaluru appointed by mutual agreement.

6.2 The losing party in any arbitration shall pay the reasonable legal costs of the other party.

6.3 This Agreement may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.
