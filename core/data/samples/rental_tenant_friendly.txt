RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement is made on 15 February 2026 between Harborview Residences LLP (the "Landlord") and Priya Nair (the "Tenant") for the flat at 22 Sea Breeze Road, Apartment 9A, Mumbai (the "Premises").

1. TERM AND RENT

1.1 The lease begins on 1 March 2026 and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months.

1.2 The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of INR 28,000 on or before the fifth day of each month.

1.3 The Tenant has a grace period of ten (10) days after the due date. If rent is still unpaid after the grace period, a one-time late fee of 2% of the monthly rent applies.

1.4 The Landlord may increase the rent only at renewal, by no more than 5%, and only after giving the Tenant sixty (60) days written notice.

1.5 At the end of the initial term, the Tenant may renew this lease for a further twelve (12) months by giving the Landlord thirty (30) days written notice. The lease does not renew unless the Tenant chooses to renew.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT

2.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to two (2) months' rent.

2.2 The Landlord shall return the deposit in full within fifteen (15) days after the Tenant vacates, together with an itemized list of any deductions for damage beyond normal wear and tear.

3. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

3.1 The Landlord is responsible for all structural, plumbing and electrical repairs and shall complete urgent repairs within forty-eight (48) hours of being notified.

3.2 The Tenant is responsible for minor repairs costing less than INR 1,000 per incident, unless the damage was caused by the Landlord or the building.

3.3 The Landlord may enter the Premises only after giving at least forty-eight (48) hours notice, except in an emergency.

3.4 The Tenant may sublet the Premises or keep a pet with the Landlord's consent, which the Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold.

4. TERMINATION

4.1 The Tenant may terminate this lease at any time by giving the Landlord thirty (30) days written notice. No termination fee is payable.

4.2 The Landlord may terminate this lease only if the Tenant has failed to pay rent for two (2) consecutive months and has not paid within thirty (30) days after written notice of the default.

4.3 If either party breaches this lease, the other party shall give written notice and the breaching party has thirty (30) days to cure the breach before any termination takes effect.

5. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

5.1 The Landlord shall indemnify the Tenant against all claims and losses caused by the Landlord's negligence or by defects in the building.

5.2 The Tenant is liable only for damage caused by the Tenant's own negligence.

6. GENERAL

6.1 This lease is governed by the laws of the State of Maharashtra and the courts of Mumbai have jurisdiction over any dispute.

6.2 Each party shall bear its own legal costs.

6.3 This lease may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.
