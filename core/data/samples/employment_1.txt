EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is made on 3 April 2026 between TechNova Solutions Pvt Ltd (the "Employer") and Rohan Verma (the "Employee") for the position of Software Engineer.

1. COMPENSATION

1.1 The Employer shall pay the Employee an annual salary of INR 12,00,000, payable in equal monthly instalments on the last working day of each month.

1.2 Any bonus is paid entirely at the Employer's discretion and the Employee has no right to a bonus in any year.

1.3 The Employer may change the Employee's role, location, working hours and compensation structure at any time by giving written notice.

2. PROBATION AND NOTICE

2.1 The first six (6) months are a probation period. During probation either party may terminate this Agreement by giving seven (7) days written notice.

2.2 After probation, the Employee shall give the Employer ninety (90) days written notice to resign, and the Employer may terminate the Employee by giving thirty (30) days written notice.

2.3 If the Employee fails to serve the full notice period, the Employee shall pay the Employer salary in lieu of the unserved notice and any recruitment costs.

2.4 The Employer may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice for misconduct, poor performance or any other reason the Employer considers sufficient.

3. TRAINING BOND

3.1 If the Employee leaves within twenty-four (24) months of joining, the Employee shall repay the Employer INR 2,00,000 as liquidated damages for training costs.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 The Employee shall keep all Employer information confidential during employment and indefinitely after it ends.

4.2 All inventions, software and works created by the Employee, whether during or outside working hours, belong to the Employer.

5. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

5.1 For twenty-four (24) months after employment ends, the Employee shall not work for or advise any business that competes with the Employer anywhere in India, and no compensation is payable for this restriction.

5.2 For twenty-four (24) months after employment ends, the Employee shall not solicit any customer or employee of the Employer.

6. LIABILITY AND DISPUTES

6.1 The Employee shall indemnify the Employer against all losses, claims and expenses arising from the Employee's acts or omissions during employment.

6.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Karnataka and the courts of Bengaluru have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute.

6.3 The Employer may amend its policies and this Agreement at any time, and the amended terms apply from the date of notice.
