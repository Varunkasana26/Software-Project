EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is made on 20 January 2026 between Greenfield Analytics Ltd (the "Employer") and Meera Iyer (the "Employee") for the position of Data Analyst.

1. TERM AND COMPENSATION

1.1 This Agreement is for a fixed term of two (2) years starting on 1 February 2026. It renews for a further one (1) year term unless either party gives the other sixty (60) days written notice that it will not renew.

1.2 The Employer shall pay the Employee a monthly salary of INR 85,000 on or before the 28th day of each month.

1.3 If the Employer pays salary late, the Employer shall pay interest on the unpaid amount at 1.5% per month until it is paid.

1.4 The Employee is entitled to twenty-four (24) days of paid leave each year, in addition to public holidays.

2. TERMINATION

2.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other party thirty (30) days written notice.

2.2 If the Employer terminates the Employee without cause, the Employer shall pay the Employee severance equal to three (3) months' salary.

2.3 If the Employer has concerns about the Employee's performance, it shall give written notice and the Employee has thirty (30) days to cure the concerns before any termination for performance takes effect.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

3.1 The Employee shall keep the Employer's confidential information secret during employment and for three (3) years after employment ends.

3.2 Works created by the Employee in the course of employment using the Employer's resources belong to the Employer. Works created independently, outside working hours and without the Employer's resources belong to the Employee.

4. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

4.1 For six (6) months after employment ends, the Employee shall not join a direct competitor in the same city. The Employer shall pay the Employee fifty percent (50%) of the last monthly salary for each month of this restriction.

5. LIABILITY AND DISPUTES

5.1 The Employer shall indemnify the Employee against claims arising from acts done by the Employee in good faith in the course of employment.

5.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes shall be resolved by arbitration in Delhi before a single arbitrator appointed by both parties, and each party shall bear its own costs.

5.3 This Agreement may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.
