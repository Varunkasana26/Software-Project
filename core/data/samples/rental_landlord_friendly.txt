RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Residential Rental Agreement is made on 1 March 2026 between Meridian Property Holdings Ltd (the "Landlord") and Arjun Mehta (the "Tenant") for the property at 14 Lakeview Apartments, Unit 5B, Pune (the "Premises").

1. TERM AND RENT

1.1 The tenancy begins on 1 April 2026 and continues for a fixed term of twelve (12) months.

1.2 The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of INR 32,000 on or before the first day of each month, by bank transfer to the account designated by the Landlord.

1.3 If rent is not received within three (3) days of the due date, the Tenant shall pay a late fee of 8% of the monthly rent, plus INR 500 for each additional day of delay.

1.4 The Landlord may increase the rent by up to 15% at any time upon fourteen (14) days written notice, including during the fixed term.

1.5 This Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms unless the Tenant gives the Landlord written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT

2.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to four (4) months' rent before taking possession.

2.2 The deposit is non-refundable if the Tenant vacates before the end of the fixed term, and the Landlord may retain the entire deposit as liquidated damages.

2.3 The Landlord shall return any remaining deposit within ninety (90) days after the Tenant vacates, after deducting any amounts the Landlord considers necessary for cleaning, painting or repairs.

3. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

3.1 The Tenant is responsible for all repairs and maintenance of the Premises, including structural, plumbing and electrical repairs, regardless of cause.

3.2 The Landlord may enter the Premises at any time without prior notice for inspection or any other reason.

3.3 The Tenant shall not sublet the Premises or keep pets without the prior written consent of the Landlord, which may be withheld at the Landlord's sole discretion.

4. TERMINATION

4.1 The Landlord may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, by giving the Tenant seven (7) days written notice.

4.2 The Tenant may terminate this Agreement only after the first six (6) months, by giving the Landlord sixty (60) days written notice and paying a termination fee equal to two (2) months' rent.

4.3 If the Tenant breaches any term and fails to cure within three (3) days after notice, the Landlord may terminate immediately and take possession of the Premises.

5. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

5.1 The Tenant shall indemnify and hold harmless the Landlord from all claims, losses, damages and expenses arising from the Tenant's use of the Premises, including claims caused by the Landlord's own negligence.

5.2 The Landlord is not liable for any loss, injury or damage to the Tenant or the Tenant's property under any circumstances.

6. GENERAL

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Maharashtra. Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Pune, and the Tenant waives the right to bring a claim in court.

6.2 The Tenant shall pay all legal costs, including the Landlord's attorney fees, incurred in enforcing this Agreement.

6.3 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.
